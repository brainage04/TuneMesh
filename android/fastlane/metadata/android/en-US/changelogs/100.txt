First public release.

- Plays the music library in a Syncthing-shared folder: albums, artists, playlists, Liked Songs, search
- Background playback with notification, lock screen and headset controls
- Download requests: paste several YouTube, YouTube Music or Spotify links at once, or share a link to TuneMesh
- Shows which device downloads requests, and per-request progress and warnings
- Onboarding for All files access and Syncthing-Fork setup
